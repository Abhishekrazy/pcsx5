# PCSX5 Reporting Governance

Use `.gemini/rules/33-reporting-audit-task.md` as the mandatory rule for durable AI workstream reporting.

The two primary artifacts are:

- `docs/audits/` for evidence and architecture audits.
- `docs/tasks/` for implementation/task completion records.

The final chat response is a concise projection of these durable artifacts, not their replacement.
